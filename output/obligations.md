## Key Obligations

Source: `input/sample_contract.txt` — Software-as-a-Service Agreement between Acme Corp ("Customer") and TechVendor Ltd. ("Vendor").

### Acme Corp (Customer)
- Pay the annual subscription fee of USD 180,000, invoiced annually in advance and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date (Section 3.1).
- Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month (Section 3.3).
- Provide written notice of non-renewal at least seven (7) days before the end of the then-current term, otherwise the Agreement automatically renews for successive twenty-four (24) month Renewal Terms (Section 2.2).
- Indemnify and hold harmless TechVendor Ltd. from third-party claims arising from Customer's use of the Services (Section 5.3).
- Customer's liability to Vendor is unlimited, including indemnification obligations, and may include direct, indirect, consequential, special, and punitive damages (Section 5.2).
- Customer Data is irrevocably assigned to Vendor (Section 4.1).
- Fees paid are non-refundable, and termination for convenience by Customer does not entitle it to a refund of prepaid fees (Sections 3.3, 6.3).
- Protect TechVendor Ltd.'s non-public business information with no less than reasonable care, for three (3) years after termination (Section 7.1).

### TechVendor Ltd. (Vendor)
- Provide Customer with access to its cloud-based workflow automation platform (the "Services") during the Term, in accordance with Schedule A (Section 1.1).
- Use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available 99.5% of the time each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance (Section 1.3).
- Maintain industry-standard administrative and technical safeguards for the Services (Section 4.3).
- Protect Acme Corp's non-public business information with the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care, for three (3) years after termination (Section 7.1).
- Is under no obligation to remind Customer of an upcoming renewal date (Section 2.2).
- Vendor's liability is limited solely to the extent Vendor elects to provide a service credit, with no other liability for any breach, outage, or data loss (Section 5.1).
- Upon termination, Customer's access to the Services ceases immediately (Section 6.3).
- Amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties (Section 9.1).
